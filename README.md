## About Us

Tomson Electronics is one of the largest commercial suppliers of electronic components in India, spearheading the Kerala Industrial Revolution. Established in Cochin and now also operating from Coimbatore, our firm has evolved to become a trusted name for high-quality electronic components and products.

We are committed to supplying premium electronics, learning kits, and resources, supporting innovators, makers, and manufacturers nationwide. With a solid reputation for quality, reliability, and professionalism, Tomson Electronics is “Run by Engineers for Engineers.”

### Our Mission
- Deliver high-quality components and products.
- Ensure speedy delivery and competitive pricing.
- Provide exceptional customer support.
- Empower engineers, makers, and students through hands-on kits, courses, codes, and documentation.

### Our Journey
Originally set up in Cochin to simplify business-to-customer transactions, we have now expanded into the electronics e-commerce space, making innovation accessible for everyone.

### What We Offer
- **Electronic Components:** Wide selection for all your project needs.
- **Learning Kits & Courses:** Practical kits and learning resources.
- **Product Documentation & Codes:** Easy-to-follow guides, codes, and support materials.
- **Occasional 3D Models:** Design files for select products.
